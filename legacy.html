Frank Koo Endo · 1923-2005

Gymnast, soldier, judge, bridge.

Frank Endo started gymnastics at 14 after watching a handstand on the parallel bars. He spent the next seven decades in service of the sport - and helped reconnect American and Japanese gymnastics after World War II.

Photo: Frank's handstand, 1939 (family archive - permission needed)
Timeline

A life in gymnastics.

  1. 1923

    Born in Wilmington, California

    Raised on Terminal Island, where his father fished and his mother worked in the cannery.

  2. 1941

    San Pedro High champion

    Won floor exercise and parallel bars at the league finals and placed at the L.A. City championships.

  3. 1942

    Incarcerated at Santa Anita and Amache

    Detained with his family during World War II. He taught gymnastics in both camps.

  4. 1945

    Champion, then soldier

    Won the Junior All-Around title in Chicago, was drafted, and trained at the Military Intelligence Service Language School at Fort Snelling.

  5. 1946

    Occupied Japan

    Served in war crimes investigation in Osaka, where the Japan Gymnastics Association took notice of his skills.

  6. 1950

    Brings the U.S. team to Japan

    Arranged the U.S. men's Olympic team's visit - Japan's first international meet at home after the war - and introduced the team to General MacArthur.

  7. 1950s

    Judge

    Joined the Southern California Gymnastics Judges Association - by his account, America's first Asian American gymnastics judge.

  8. 1958

    Founds Frank Endo Company confirm year

    Began importing Japanese-made gymnastics shoes, then grips, supports and competition wear.

  9. 1959

    Trampoline comes to Japan

    With inventor George Nissen, organized 14 trampoline exhibitions across Japan.

  10. 1972

    Head judge, U.S. Nationals

    Named head judge at the U.S. National Championships in Statesboro, Georgia.

  11. 1984

    Los Angeles Olympics

    Vault official, and announcer for the Japanese team's Southern California exhibitions.

  12. 1990

    Japan Gymnastics Hall of Fame

    Awarded the Korosho by the Japan Gymnastics Association.

  13. 2001

    USA Gymnastics Hall of Fame

    Inducted with a Lifetime Achievement award.

  14. 2005

    Passes at 82

    The company continues under his family in Gardena.

Recognition

Honors

  • 1990

    Korosho - Japan Gymnastics Hall of Fame

    Japan Gymnastics Association. Described as the first person living outside Japan to receive it.

  • 2001

    USA Gymnastics Hall of Fame

    Lifetime Achievement. View inductee page

  • 2000

    The Frank Endo SCGJA Award

    Created by the Southern California Gymnastics Judges Association, given yearly to the region's top judge.

  • 1993-

    International Gymnastics Hall of Fame

    Board member; nominated five Japanese Olympic champions for induction. About IGHOF
