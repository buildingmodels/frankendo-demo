Our story

A lifetime in the service of gymnastics.

Gymnast, soldier, judge, interpreter - and the bridge between American and Japanese gymnastics after the war.

Frank Endo judged for more than fifty years, officiated at the 1984 Los Angeles Olympics, and is honored in the USA Gymnastics Hall of Fame and Japan's Gymnastics Hall of Fame.
